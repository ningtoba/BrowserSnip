  exactly the class the odp lesson predicts:
  * Word/Excel pages are XML, but XML in UTF-16 with a BOM. zipTexts decoded every part as
    UTF-8 — every real character became U+FFFD — and parseXml then blamed the file:
    “That XPS page is not readable XML.” A package that opens in the Windows viewer is
    refused with the WRONG CAUSE named (bar 2).

    The reader compared localName against lowercase 'glyphs', a shape only this repo’s own
    fixture invents. The WPF file, UTF-8 and parsing fine, came back
    “no text runs this reader can recover, so every page would come out blank” — its words
    in the package, the denial on the card, in the same breath.
  Both halves of the corpus pre-fix: nothing converted.
