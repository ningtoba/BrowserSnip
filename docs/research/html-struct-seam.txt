painted behind its glyphs, the list gutter sized from the widest marker) NOT applied —
against the route as it stands today, i.e. with `markStructuredLists`' temporary
`level + 1` gutter bump already removed, so the difference these two sections show is
the seam's own and nothing else. Read:

  * `bands` — a `#f1f1ec` fill of 0 dark px inside its own rectangle is the `<pre>` block
    whose text was painted over; the after section's bands carry their glyphs;

    for wide markers only.
